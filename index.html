Shop Notes // Read Before Checkout

THE DETAILS, CLEARLY.

Quick answers on fit, prints, care, shipping, and choosing the right piece.

How should I choose my size?

Check the Size Guide before ordering. For a relaxed oversized look, compare the garment measurements with a tee or hoodie you already like. If you're between sizes, use the fit preference as your guide.

How do I keep the artwork looking fresh?

Turn printed garments inside out, wash cold with similar colors, use a gentle cycle, and avoid ironing directly over the print. Air-drying is recommended to help preserve the graphic.

What are the sticker packs like?

Sticker packs are normal die-cut decals—not holographic—designed for notebooks, laptop covers, bottles, and other smooth surfaces. Clean and dry the surface before applying.

Are phone cases part of the drop?

Yes. Phone cases are listed as their own product category. Check the product details and device compatibility before ordering so the case matches your phone model.

What does the product card include?

Each card shows the product name, design description, price comparison, product type, and available size or item option. Open Details for the product's expanded view.

How do I browse the anime collections?

Use the search bar or category filters to narrow the catalog by product type or anime keywords. Clear the filter if no matches appear.
